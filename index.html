Privacy Policy

All Tiled Up
Last updated: September 21, 2026

All Tiled Up is a tile and map editor. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to use the app.
  • Your maps, tiles, and settings are stored on your device.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not upload your artwork or projects to our servers.
  • On Android Free, you may optionally watch a rewarded ad for a single export. Pro has no ads.

What stays on your device

The app saves your work locally so you can pick up where you left off. That can include painted maps, presets, imported images, and app settings. This information stays on your phone, tablet, or computer until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information through normal use of the editor. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the app.

If you contact us by email (see below), we only see what you choose to send in that message.

Ads (Android Free only)

On the free Android build, when you hit an export gate you can choose to watch a short rewarded ad for one successful download, or buy Pro. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Watching an ad is optional; the editor itself does not show ads while you work.

AdMob may collect or process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and standard ad-performance data under Google’s policies. We do not use that data to identify you personally, and we do not receive your maps or tiles through ads.

Pro removes all ads. Pro is a one-time purchase on Google Play (currently $4.99) and on itch.io for desktop.

Internet use

The editor works offline. The app may use the internet in a few limited cases:

  • Checking for app updates (Google Play on Android; optional desktop update check that opens the itch.io page).
  • Processing a Pro purchase through Google Play or itch.io. Payments are handled by those stores, not by us.
  • Loading a rewarded ad when you choose that option on Android Free.

Using the editor does not send your maps or tiles to us.

Exports and sharing

When you export files, you choose where they go—your device, a share sheet, cloud storage, and so on. Anything you share after that is up to you and the service you use.

Children

All Tiled Up is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Because your projects live on your device, you can remove them by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top will change when we do.

Contact
